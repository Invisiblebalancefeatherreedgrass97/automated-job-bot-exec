## ⚙️ Configuration

After installation, you can customize the bot to fit your needs:

- **Search Keywords**: Enter the job titles or skills you're looking for (e.g., "software engineer", "data analyst")
- **Location**: Set your preferred city or region
- **Search Frequency**: Choose how often the bot checks for new jobs (e.g., every 1 hour, every 6 hours, daily)
- **Job Boards**: Select which job sites to search (e.g., Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor)

To access settings, open the application and click on the "Settings" tab.

## 🎯 Features

- **Automated Job Search**: The bot continuously scans multiple job boards for new listings matching your criteria
- **Customizable Filters**: Narrow down results by salary range, job type, company size, and more
- **Email Notifications**: Receive alerts when new jobs are found (requires email setup)
- **Cross-Platform Support**: Works on Windows, with versions available for macOS and Linux
- **Lightweight Interface**: Uses minimal system resources and runs in the background
- **Resume Integration**: Automatically attach your resume to applications (requires resume file)
- **Application Tracking**: Keep a log of jobs you've applied to and their status

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem**: The application won't start.
**Solution**: Ensure your antivirus software isn't blocking it. Add an exception for `automated-job-bot-exec` if needed.

**Problem**: No jobs are found.
**Solution**: Check your search keywords and location. Try broadening your criteria (e.g., use "developer" instead of "senior software engineer").

**Problem**: Email notifications aren't working.
**Solution**: Verify your email settings in the configuration. Make sure you've entered a valid email address and SMTP server details.
